## WIO (Working Ideas Optional) - Technology That Might Work

### BreatheMinder™
**Status**: App Development
**Tagline**: "Because you might forget"

An app that sends you regular notifications reminding you to breathe. For people who apparently need this.

Features:
- Hourly breath reminders
- "Are you still breathing?" check-ins
- Congratulatory messages for successful breathing
- Annual statistics: "You breathed 8,409,600 times this year!"

**Market Potential**: Anxious people and those who overthink everything

### Current Activity Tracker
**Status**: Fully Functional (Unfortunately)
**Tagline**: "Tells you what you're currently doing (which you already know)"

Revolutionary app that uses your phone's sensors to tell you what you're doing right now. Information you already possess.

Features:
- "You are sitting" notifications
- "You are walking" updates
- "You are holding your phone" alerts (while reading the alert on your phone)
- Completely redundant statistics

**Market Potential**: People who question their own perception of reality
